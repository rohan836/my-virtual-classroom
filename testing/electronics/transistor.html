A tiny valve for electric current

A transistor works like a super-fast water tap controlled by electricity instead of your hand. A tiny trickle of signal at the middle leg (Base or Gate) opens a floodgate for a much bigger current.

law
Ic = β · Ib for BJTs (tiny Base current controls large Collector current). MOSFETs use Gate voltage (Vgs > Vth).
try
Toggle the Base Switch, test the Automatic Dark Sensor by dimming daylight, or compare NPN, PNP, MOSFET, and Photo types.
notice
With zero moving parts, a transistor can flip ON and OFF billions of times per second — or magnify a whisper into loud music.
in life
Microchips inside smartphones and laptops pack billions of microscopic transistors to calculate and store data.
found by
John Bardeen, Walter Brattain, and William Shockley invented the first transistor at Bell Labs in 1947.
