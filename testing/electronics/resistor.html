Notebook: key points to copy

  1. Resistor: a component that provides resistance and limits electric current.
  2. Current (I): charge flow per second, measured in A or mA (1 A = 1,000 mA).
  3. Voltage (V): energy per unit charge. Resistance (R) is measured in Ω (1 kΩ = 1,000 Ω).
  4. Ohm's Law: V = I × R and I = V ÷ R. Use the voltage across the resistor.
  5. 4-Band Code: two digits, multiplier, tolerance. Red–Red–Brown–Gold = 220 Ω ±5%.
  6. LED Safety: in a series circuit, current is equal throughout. Example: (6 − 2) ÷ 220 ≈ 18.2 mA. Switch off before changing parts.

Takeaway: Choose the right resistor to control current safely without exceeding the LED or resistor ratings.
