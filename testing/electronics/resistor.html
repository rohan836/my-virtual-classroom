Section 9 · fig. 09

Notebook: key points to copy

  1. Resistor: limits electric current and transfers energy into heat.
  2. Units: Voltage in V, Current in A/mA (1 A = 1,000 mA), Resistance in Ω (1 kΩ = 1,000 Ω).
  3. Ohm's Law: V = I × R and I = (V − Vf) ÷ R.
  4. 4-Band Code: Red–Red–Brown–Gold = 220 Ω ±5%.
  5. LED Polarity: Anode (+) = longer leg; Cathode (−) = shorter leg / flat rim edge.

Takeaway: Pick the right resistor to protect the LED and stay within the resistor's 0.25 W rating.
